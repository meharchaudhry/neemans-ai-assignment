## What happened
Sales of Cloud Sneaker - Grey dropped by 75% in Week 8 in Delhi and Mumbai, falling from 120 units to 30 units per week.

## Root cause(s) identified
1. Vendor shipment delay of sole foam (9 days delay) caused a total warehouse stockout at Delhi-DC1 in Week 8.
2. Marketing spend was concurrently cut by 80% (from ₹25,000 to ₹5,000) as budget was reallocated to Trail Runner - Olive.

## Recommended corrective actions
1. **Ops**: Establish buffer stock for high-velocity SKUs at Delhi-DC1 to absorb vendor raw-material delays.
2. **Marketing**: Re-instantiate ad budget for Cloud Sneaker - Grey following restock completion in Week 10.
